THE PERSON BEHIND THE NAME

Who Is Fizogon?

Fizogon is an independent online music artist and digital creator, also known by the names Fiz, Fizo and Tys. The name brings together several creative interests, including music, programming, digital design and game development.

Musically, Fizogon explores sounds associated with hyperpop, digicore, scenecore and emo rap, combining electronic production with melodic vocals and emotionally driven themes.

Outside music, Fizogon works on Roblox game development, Luau scripting and web design. These projects cover both the technical and visual sides of creating things online, from interactive game systems to custom websites.

This website serves as a central destination for official information, photographs, music and creative projects associated with the Fizogon name.
