02 / ABOUT

WHO IS FIZOGON?

The artist behind the music and the developer behind the code.

OFFICIAL PROFILE

Hey, I'm Fizogon, also known online as Fiz, Fizo and Tys.

I'm an independent music artist creating hyperpop-inspired music that combines bright electronic production with darker, emotional writing. My styles include hyperpop, digicore, scenecore and emo rap, alongside nostalgic internet sounds and emotional electronic music.

I'm also a Roblox game developer and web designer. I write gameplay systems in Luau and build websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I started scripting on 2 June 2023 and continue learning about cybersecurity and ethical hacking.

My creative identity is shaped by internet culture, online music communities, programming and the projects I build. This website brings my music, development work and online identity together in one place.
